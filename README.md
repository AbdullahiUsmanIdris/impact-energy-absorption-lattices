# impact-energy-absorption-lattices

# Mechanical Response and Energy Absorption of Lattice Structures
## Project Overview
This project investigates the mechanical response and energy
absorption behavior of 3D- printed lattice structures under
quasi - static compression . The objective is to establish an
experimental - numerical framework that can later be extended
to dynamic and impact loading conditions .
## Motivation
Lightweight lattice structures are increasingly used in energy - absorbing applications such as crashworthiness and
protective systems . However , their mechanical response is
highly dependent on geometry and deformation mechanisms .
This project aims to understand these mechanisms through
controlled experiments and finite element modeling .
## Scope
- Design of lattice unit cells with controlled relative density
- Fabrication using FDM ( Prusa printer )
- Quasi - static compression testing
- Finite element modeling ( ANSYS / Abaqus )
- Comparison of force - displacement and energy absorption metrics
## Methods
### Experimental
- Specimens fabricated from PLA using fixed printing parameters
- Compression tests performed under displacement control
- Force - displacement data used to compute absorbed energy
### Numerical
- Geometry idealization and meshing of lattice structures
- Linear elastic or elastic - plastic material models
- Boundary conditions matched to experiments
- Numerical results compared against experimental data
## Key Outputs
- Force - displacement curves
- Energy absorption metrics
- Deformation and failure mode analysis
- Experimental - numerical correlation
